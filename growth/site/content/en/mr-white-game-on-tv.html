“Mr. White” is the name many players give to a secret-word party game, after its most famous role: the player who gets no word and has to bluff. Everyone else gets a word, gives one clue, and votes out whoever sounds off. If Mr. White is caught, they get one guess at the word to steal the win.

To play it on a TV, Mish Ana! is a free secret-word game for Android TV, Google TV or any browser where each player’s word goes to their own phone. Its no-word role is the Mole, and it’s in every default game: the Mole’s phone shows “YOU’RE THE MOLE!” instead of a word.

What is the Mr. White game?

It is a social-deduction word game for groups. The name comes from the Mr. White role in Undercover, a phone party game by Yanstar Studio, and it has stuck, especially among French-speaking players who say “on fait un Mr White ?”. The idea works the same way in every version:

  • Most players share the same secret word (for example PIZZA).
  • In some versions, one or more players get a close but different word (PASTA) and don’t know it.
  • Mr. White gets no word, knows it, and bluffs from the clues he hears.
  • Everyone says one clue in turn, then the group votes someone out and their role is revealed.

How to play Mr. White with paper (no app)

You can play the classic version with a game master and folded papers:

  1. The game master picks two close words (cat / dog, beach / pool) and writes the majority word on most papers, the other word on one paper and nothing on one paper.
  2. Everyone draws a paper in secret. The game master doesn’t play.
  3. Going around the circle, each player says one word linked to their paper. The blank player bluffs.
  4. Everyone points at the suspect on three. The most-pointed player reveals their paper.
  5. If the blank player is caught, they get one guess at the majority word.

It works, but someone has to sit out as game master, papers get seen, and nobody tracks scores. That is what an app, or a TV, fixes.

Playing it on the TV with Mish Ana!

Mish Ana! turns the TV into the game master. It shows a QR code; each player scans it and gets their word privately on their own phone, in the browser, with nothing to install. The TV picks who speaks, runs the timers, collects the votes from the phones and does the big reveal.

Tips if you are Mr. White (the Mole)

  • Hope to speak late: by the fourth clue you can usually guess the theme.
  • Give a clue that fits many words in the theme (“you share it with friends”).
  • Vote like the majority, and keep your guess ready in case you are caught.
What’s the Mole? What’s the Undercover? In Mish Ana!, the Mole is the Mr. White-style role: no word, a “YOU’RE THE MOLE!” card, vague clues, and one last guess at the word if caught. The Undercover is an optional extra role: a different word (PASTA while everyone has PIZZA) that they don’t know is different. Groups can add Undercovers or mix both roles. Who is Mr. White in the word game? Mr. White is the player who receives no secret word. They know it, listen to the other players’ clues, and try to bluff a believable clue. If they are voted out, they can win by guessing the majority word. Can Mr. White win? Yes, in two ways: by surviving until too few majority-word players are left to win, or by guessing the majority word right after being voted out.
