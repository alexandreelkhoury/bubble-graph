Short answer: open the app’s page in Google Play on your phone, signed in with the same Google account as your TV. On Android, tap the arrow next to Install (or “Install on more devices”) and tick your TV. On an iPhone or a computer, open the page on play.google.com in the browser, tap Install and choose your TV. The app downloads to the TV on its own.

This works for any app that supports Android TV or Google TV, on any TV or streamer with Google TV or Android TV: many Sony, TCL and Hisense models, older Philips models, the Google TV Streamer and the Chromecast with Google TV (no longer sold, still supported). It saves typing with the remote. The steps below use Mish Ana! as the example, but they are the same for every app.

From an Android phone (Play Store app)

Google’s own steps: install apps on multiple devices.

  1. Open the Google Play Store app and search for the app (for example “Mish Ana”).
  2. Check that the account in the top corner is the one your TV uses.
  3. On the app page, tap the down arrow next to Install (some phones show “Install on more devices” instead).
  4. Tick your TV and tap Install.
  5. On the TV, the app appears in your apps within a minute or two (Apps tab, or “Your apps” on Google TV).

From an iPhone or a computer (play.google.com)

Google’s steps: install apps from the Play Store website.

  1. Open a browser (Safari, Chrome…) and go to play.google.com.
  2. Sign in with the Google account your TV uses (top right).
  3. Search for the app and open its page.
  4. Click Install, choose your TV in the device dropdown, then click Install again.
  5. Confirm. The TV downloads the app by itself while it is on or in standby with network.

On the TV itself, with the remote or your voice

See Google TV Help and, for older models, Android TV Help.

  1. On Google TV, go to the Apps tab and choose Search for apps. On older Android TV, open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Type or say the app’s name (hold the Google Assistant button on the remote and say “Mish Ana”).
  3. Select the app and press Install.

Your TV isn’t in the list? Fixes

  • Different Google account. The phone or browser must be signed in to the account set up on the TV. On the TV: Settings → Accounts & sign-in.
  • The TV doesn’t have Google Play. Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Amazon Fire TV and Apple TV can’t install apps from Google Play; they use their own stores. Note that Philips TVs from 2026 run Titan OS rather than Google TV.
  • The app isn’t made for TV. The developer chooses which devices an app supports; the app page shows which of your devices are compatible.
  • The TV hasn’t been used lately. Google Play only lists devices linked to your account and active in the last 30 days. Turn the TV on, open the Apps tab or the Play Store once, then try again.
  • Country or parental controls. Play only shows apps available in your Play country, and parental controls can block installs (Google Play Help).
  • Still nothing after a few minutes. Make sure the TV is connected to the internet, then restart it (unplug for 30 seconds).

Can I install apps on Google TV from my iPhone? Yes. Open play.google.com in Safari, sign in with the Google account your TV uses, open the app’s page, tap Install and choose your TV. The iPhone doesn’t need any Google app. Why doesn’t my TV show up when I tap Install? Usually because the phone and the TV use different Google accounts, the TV hasn’t been used in the last 30 days, or the app doesn’t support TVs. Samsung, LG, Fire TV and Apple TV never appear because they don’t use Google Play. Does Chromecast work? The Chromecast with Google TV (discontinued in 2025 but still supported) and the Google TV Streamer install apps from Google Play. Older Chromecasts without a remote can’t install apps; they only receive casts. Can I install TV apps from the Google TV app on my phone? Use the Google Play Store instead: the remote-install option lives in Play (the app on Android, or play.google.com in a browser). The Google TV phone app works as a remote. Do I have to keep the TV on? The TV needs to be connected to the internet to download the app. If it’s off, the install usually starts the next time it is turned on.
