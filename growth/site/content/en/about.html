Key facts

The name

“JAMAA” (said ja-MAA; Arabic جماعة) means “the gang, the gathering”: the people in the room, all playing together on one TV.

The games

Mish Ana! is game #1: Its name (مش أنا!) is Lebanese Arabic for “Not me!”, which is exactly what every player says when the group accuses them. In French it’s « Pas moi ! ».

How a Mish Ana! game works

The TV shows a QR code and a room code. Each player scans it and joins in their phone’s browser with a nickname. Everyone gets the same secret word on their own phone, except the Mole, whose phone shows “YOU’RE THE MOLE!” and no word; an optional Undercover gets a slightly different word. Players give one spoken clue each, vote on their phones, and the TV reveals the role of the player voted out. Full rules: how to play (en français).
