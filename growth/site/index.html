Which TVs does it work on?

Any Android TV or Google TV with the Google Play Store: many Sony, TCL and Hisense TVs, some older Philips models, the Google TV Streamer and the Chromecast with Google TV (discontinued, still supported).

Quick check: if your TV has the Google Play Store, it works.

My TV is a Samsung, LG, Fire TV or Apple TV. Can we still play?

Yes, in a browser. The TV app needs Google Play, so it isn't available on those TVs. Open play.mishana.workers.dev/tv on a laptop or computer, plug it into the TV with HDMI (or gather round the screen), and phones join the same way.

I'm on an iPhone. Can I still install it on my TV?

Yes. Open the Google Play page in your browser, sign in with the Google account your TV uses, and choose your TV. Or install it on the TV directly: search “Mish Ana” in the Apps tab.

How much does it cost?

Mish Ana! is free to play, with no account to create. Optional in-app purchases may add extra content.

Do my friends need to download anything?

No. They scan the QR code on the TV and play in their phone's browser. iPhone and Android both work.

How long does it take to set up?

Installing is like any TV app: a few taps from your phone. After that, a game starts as soon as everyone has scanned the QR code. No accounts, no controllers to pair, and one round teaches the rules.

Do we need internet?

Yes. The TV and the phones talk to each other through the internet, so each one needs a connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

Is it OK for kids?

Mish Ana! is made for teens and adults, and it's often played with the whole family. Turn on the family-friendly word filter, and Beginner mode shows everyone their role. Players only type a nickname, and rooms are deleted automatically.

How many people can play?

3 to 12 players. Everyone needs their own phone.

Which languages?

English, French and Arabic, with Lebanese word packs. The Arabic version is fully right-to-left.

What do you do with our data?

Very little. Players type a nickname, and the room is deleted soon after the game ends. No accounts, no ad trackers. This website counts visits and button clicks without cookies. Read the privacy policy.

About Mish Ana!

Mish Ana! is a secret-word party game for Android TV and Google TV: 3–12 players join from their phones by scanning a QR code, get a secret word, and find the player whose word is different.

Name
“Mish Ana!” (مش أنا!) is Lebanese Arabic for “Not me!”, what everyone says when they’re accused.
Genre
Party game, social deduction, word game
Plays on
Android TV and Google TV (Google Play), or any web browser
Roles
Civilians share a word. The Mole has a slightly different word and isn’t told. The Blank has no word and bluffs.
Price
Free to play. No ads, no accounts.
