# Creative review — Mish Ana! ad scripts (v1 → v2)

Reviewed 2026-10-04 against the game repo (`bubble-graph/README.md`, `docs/SPEC.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`, `shared/i18n/*.json`, `word-packs/`). Sections 1, 2, 3 and 5 of `SCRIPTS.md` are rewritten in place. Section 4 is untouched; notes on it are below.

**Before anything else:** the README says the Play Store release (M5) hasn't started and the TV app hasn't been tested on a real device yet. No ad can run until the listing is live, and the end card needs a real recording of the install flow, which only exists once the listing does.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The strategy is sound: the reveal angle first, a reusable 9.5 s body, a shared end card, a clean naming/UTM scheme. But v1 showed a game that doesn't exist. It had a "MOLE!" stamp, phones that flag the Mole in amber, a vote by pointing, a Mole who wins after one wrong vote, and PIZZA/PASTA in Arabic, where that pair isn't in any pack. It also invented a Play Store screen. Several hooks wasted their first second (static shots, type-on text, silence), and the test plan (6 filmed angles + 3 motion ads, two platforms) cost too much for a solo owner and couldn't separate angle from hook. v2 fixes all of that.

## Critique notes by area (short)

- **Hook strength (first 1.5 s).** Strongest: A_H1, A_H5 (new), B_H1, E_H1, A_H4 (new), C_M3 (it gives the viewer a task). Weakest: C_H1 and D_H1, even after the fixes, because they open on an "intent" or "boredom" shot with no faces reacting. B_H3's split screen leaves faces too small at 9:16. Expect motion-only ads to hook 5–10 points lower than filmed ones, so judge them against each other, not against UGC.
- **Sound-off clarity.** BODY-1 now teaches the game in captions alone: scan → secret word, one is different → one clue each, out loud → vote on your phone → OUT, he was the Mole. The one concept that still needs the eye is "the Mole doesn't know". A_H2 now carries it.
- **Pacing.** Most beats are 0.5–1.5 s. The remaining slow spots are the 3 s talking head in C_H1 (keep the fast take) and the 4 s end card. If hold rate dips at the end, cut the EC to 3.2 s by merging the "Pick your TV" and "Installing" beats.
- **CTA friction.** Watching on a phone and installing on a TV is the funnel's biggest leak. It needs an Android phone, the Play Store app, and a TV on the same Google account. Mitigations: Android-only targeting, "Android TV & Google TV" on screen early in angle C/E ads, the search-on-TV fallback, and a landing page for TikTok.
- **Filming guide.** Good on releases, branding and the real-rounds-first approach. It lacked the game setup (random pairs and roles, Blank at 5+, timers), so the PIZZA/PASTA shots were luck. 3 h for 33 shots plus real rounds was optimistic: now 4 h with ★ priorities.
- **Budget realism.** v1 implied about 9 ads × $30–50 on two platforms (about $500–900) before learning anything. v2 learns the same in €280 on one platform.

## Notes for section 4 (MA_A_M1, MA_E_M2, MA_C_M3, in production now)

Not edited, as instructed. Apply these in the Remotion build if you can. Items 1–3 are accuracy problems that a player would notice.

1. **"MOLE!" stamp does not exist** (A_M1 9.0–11.0, C_M3 11.0–13.5). The game stamps **OUT** at −8° on the voted tile, then the card flips to the orange `#FF8A3D` Mole card with the domino mask and "…the Mole! Nice catch." Keep "IT WAS YOU?!" as the caption. Swap only the stamp word and add the orange flip.
2. **Phones must not mark the Mole** (A_M1 0.0–2.4, E_M2 6.0–9.0). The real phone shows a face-down card in the player's colour → hold → "Your secret word" + word. The Mole's card is styled exactly like the others. Keep the glitch/wobble/amber as an **effect on the frame or a ring around the phone**, not as a recoloured amber screen. "YOUR SECRET WORD: PIZZA" is fine if styled as the card face (the real label is "Your secret word").
3. **Language versions:** PIZZA/PASTA isn't in the Arabic packs. AR versions of A_M1, E_M2 and C_M3 should use **بيتزا / برغر** (A_M1 AR hook: «كلّن معن بيتزا…» / «إلّا هوّي.»; C_M3's clues need Arabic equivalents that fit pizza/burger, e.g. "جبنة", "فرن", "شوكة", "كاتشب"). FR versions use **PIZZA / PÂTES**, and C_M3's FR caption should explain the fork joke with « pâtes ».
4. **End card** (all three): don't ship a redrawn Play Store screen with an "Install ▾" sheet and a "This phone" row. A TV-only app has no "This phone" option, and a fake store UI is a likely rejection. Until the real recording exists, use an abstract phone screen (icon + "Install on your TV"). Add the line **"Or search “Mish Ana” on your TV"** under the badge, and say "Free to play".
5. **E_M2:** the "Start → magenta flood races across all phones" beat should show cards flipping in each player's colour, not one magenta flood (that's the "Your turn" effect, in the speaker's colour). "Lobby + QR + room code" and the three chips are accurate.
6. **C_M3:** with 5 players the default game also deals a **Blank**. The puzzle is fine as a simplification, but don't write "1 Mole among 5" anywhere. Clues at 1.2 s each (2.0–8.0) are the slowest stretch in the three ads; 0.9 s each would end the clues at 6.5 s. "Comment your guess" is the right CTA for this one.
7. **All three:** the hook caption must be visible on frame 1, not built in over the first beat. Check the right-rail safe zone on the 3+2 phone fan in A_M1.
8. **Expectations:** without faces, these will likely hook lower than the filmed ads. In Round 1, compare them with each other and use them to rank angles, not formats.

## Round 1 launch plan

**Pre-flight (must all be true):** the app is live on Google Play for Android TV. The attribution test is done (tagged link → installed on your TV from your phone → checked Play Console 48 h later). The end card uses the real store recording, or the abstract placeholder. Releases are signed. Section 5 is checked.

| **Audience** | One English-speaking geo (UK suggested; CA is the alternative; skip the US for Round 1, where CPMs are highest), 18–44, **Android devices only**, broad + interests "board games / party games" in one audience, the same for all 5 ad sets |

**Decision rules**

- **Day 3 (72 h, ≥ €25 and ≥ 2,000 impressions per ad):** turn off any ad with a 3 s hook rate < 20 % or a link CTR < 0.4 %. Don't move its budget anywhere else (keep the test clean).
- **Day 7:** rank the survivors by **cost per Play Store visit**, among ads with hook rate ≥ 25 % and CTR ≥ 0.6 % (≥ 40 clicks each).
  - **Winner** → duplicate into a scaling ad set at €20/day. Raise it 20–30 % every 72 h while cost per visit stays within +20 %.
  - **Winning angle** (A, B, C or E) gets 70 % of the Round 2 budget, and the runner-up 30 %. Round 2 = 3 new hooks on the winner + 2 on the runner-up (A: H2/H4/H5; B: H4/H5/H2), with the Round 1 winner as the control. Same €40/day, 7 days.
- **Diagnose before killing a high hook rate:** low hold → shorten the body. Good hold but low CTR → fix the end card ("Free to play", show the install step earlier). Good CTR but no install lift → put "Android TV & Google TV" on screen in the first 3 s.
- **Stop rule:** if the best ad's CTR is < 0.5 %, or Play Console shows no TV-install lift in the geo after €150 and 150+ store visits, pause. The problem is the install path, not the creative: build the landing page with the "search on your TV" fallback, then rerun the 2 best ads.
- **Next:** Round 3 = the winning hooks in French, in France (large Android TV base through ISP boxes; check those boxes have the Play Store). Then the December family run (F_H3, AR/EN, Lebanon + diaspora), filmed in November.

**First-month spend estimate:** Round 1 €280 + Round 2 €280 + scaling €20–30/day ≈ **€750–850**, with a stop at €150 if installs don't move.
