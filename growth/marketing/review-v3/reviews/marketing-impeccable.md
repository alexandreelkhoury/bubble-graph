# Marketing review v3 · lens: IMPECCABLE (production quality)

Scope: landing `growth/site` (EN/FR/AR at 390 and 1440, privacy, 404, OG), Play Store (`growth/marketing/play-store`: icon, feature graphic, TV banner, screenshot template), video end cards (`growth/video/out/final`). Checked against: palette B (`growth/video/src/brand.ts`), `docs/DESIGN.md`, `review-v2/DECISIONS.md`, Google Play preview-asset specs (support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151, fetched today) and TV quality TV-G4 / TV-BN (developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/tv-app-quality). Contrast is WCAG 2.x, computed from the hex values.

## 1. Verdict

The landing page is close to production quality. It uses palette B, the type ramp is clean, it holds up at 390 and 1440, honours reduced motion, avoids the usual AI-design tells, and the pre-launch copy is honest. **It is not ready to ship until the build is fixed.** Every page still contains `{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}` / `{{OWNER_NAME}}`. Guide FAQ links have the literal href `{{BROWSER_URL}}`. The 404 button is cream on magenta (2.89:1). FR and AR visitors see an English hero video.

The Play Store set is **not submittable**. There are no TV screenshots, and TV-G4 requires at least one *unaltered* one. The feature graphic, TV banner, OG image and frame template still use the old app night palette (`#120A1F` / `#FF3D8B`), not palette B. They also skip decision #19 (TV + 2 phones). The video end cards are good. Two small leftovers remain: a second, different URL shown inside the end card, and a URL treatment that differs between the ads and the film.

Audit health: A11y 3 · Perf 3 · Responsive 3 · Theming 2 · Anti-patterns 3 = **14/20 (Good: fix the weak dimensions, theming first)**.

## 3. Keep (already excellent)

1. **Pre-launch honesty system.** `site.config.json playStoreLive:false` + `content/prelaunch.json` rewrites every CTA, hint, FAQ and chip so nothing promises Google Play before it exists. The "Send the link to my laptop or TV" label swap on phones is smart and causes no layout shift.
2. **Palette B on the landing.** Tokens are 1:1 with `brand.ts`. Contrast is strong everywhere it matters: muted copy 10.10:1 on bg, 8.42:1 on surface; magenta CTA label is ink at 6.04:1; amber 10.29:1. The lit stage background matches the ads.
3. **Motion discipline.** Reveal is visible by default and only "armed" once observed off-screen, with a 1.5 s failsafe. The reel and hero video pause off-screen, with a WCAG 2.2.2 pause button. Reduced motion and data-saver both give static media. That is production quality.
4. **RTL by construction.** Logical properties throughout, `<bdi>` around the brand, mirrored arrows, no letter-spacing on Arabic, pre-rendered `/ar/` with correct `lang`/`dir`/hreflang/canonical/`og:locale ar_LB`, and a Lebanese-dialect voice.
5. **Video end cards (v2).** One CTA, a typeable URL held still, an Arabic wordmark on M6, brand chip, honest "Google TV app coming soon". Keep them, apart from #19, #27 and #28.
