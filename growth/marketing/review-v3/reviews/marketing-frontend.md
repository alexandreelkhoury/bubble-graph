# Marketing: frontend-design review (v3)

Lens: visual craft (frontend-design skill). Surfaces: landing `growth/site/` (EN/FR/AR at 390 and 1440), Play icon, feature graphic, TV banner, video end cards.
Mockups (HTML sources in `mockups/src/`, rendered with headless Chrome + Playwright via `mockups/src/render.mjs`):
- `mockups/mkt-hero-desktop-1440.png`: desktop hero, 1440×900
- `mockups/mkt-hero-mobile-390.png` and `mockups/mkt-hero-mobile-390-ar.png`: mobile hero, 390×844 @2x, EN and AR (RTL)
- `mockups/mkt-feature-graphic-1024x500.png` and `mockups/mkt-feature-graphic-compare.png`: new graphic with Play's play-button zone drawn on it, plus current vs new at 256 px thumbnail size
- `mockups/mkt-icon-proposal-512.png` and `mockups/mkt-icon-compare.png`: icon on light and dark launchers at 72/48/32 px

Capture caveat: `landing-*-390-fold.png` show the page scrolled about 474 css px down. That comes from the capture, not from the site. `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` (site.css:45) animates `scrollTo(0,0)` from the bottom of a 7000 px page, and that hadn't finished within the 800 ms wait in `landing.mjs`. I reviewed the top crop of the full-page shots instead.

## 1. Verdict

The landing is polished and on-brand: palette B, Cairo Black, real player shapes, the 15-second reel and the hold-to-peek demo are all well made. But the hero is still a generic SaaS 50/50 split (copy on the left, a small 518 px TV on the right), and its key visual is an English-only video, so the FR and AR heroes show "Give one-word clues." and "…but one is different.". The hook, one word is different, only exists inside that video. The static frame never shows PIZZA next to PASTA.

The Play feature graphic doesn't implement approved decision #19 (TV + 2 phones). It is a centred wordmark in the old night palette, and it sits right in Play's play-button zone. The icon's dark tile disappears on dark launchers.

Fixes: an asymmetric "stage" hero with a real TV screen plus a PIZZA/PASTA phone pair, the ads' tilted hot-word box on the H1, and a localized hero caption layer.

## 3. Keep (already excellent)

1. **The 15-second reel** (`.reel`, `landing-en-1440.png` y≈1000-1720): CSS-built TV lobby + phone joining, step rail with progress bars, pause control. It's honest, uses the real UI language (QR, room code, player shapes) and reads in 2 s.
2. **Hold-to-peek demo** (`.deal`): an interactive toy that teaches the core privacy mechanic with Mole/Blank re-deals. Rare on landing pages and exactly right for this game.
3. **Player shapes as the visual system** (`.av--star/tri/diamond/hex`): circle, square, star, triangle, diamond, hexagon in the lifted palette. Consistent across the TV, the reel, the cards and the end cards. Keep them as the brand's "cast".
4. **Palette B + grain + Cairo Black** at `letter-spacing:-.03em`, line-height .98: warm, lit, distinctive, and the Arabic setting (line-height 1.25, no tracking) is handled properly.
5. **Ad end cards' structure** (wordmark → product → CTA pill → typeable URL, CTA centred around y 1200, bottom 25% clear of TikTok/Reels UI). Correct for the platforms; only the room-code emphasis needs a tweak (#19).
