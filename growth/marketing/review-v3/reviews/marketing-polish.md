# Marketing: polish review (lens: make-interfaces-feel-better)

Scope: landing `growth/site/` (EN/FR/AR at 390 and 1440, privacy, 404), Play Store graphics, video end cards. I applied the skill's checklist: concentric radius, optical alignment, shadows vs borders, enter/exit and stagger, tabular numbers, font smoothing, text-wrap, image outlines, scale on press, no `transition: all`, hit areas. I also checked font loading, hand-off, in-app webview and RTL. Values were measured from the files (fontTools, ffprobe/ffmpeg pixel sampling, contrast maths), not guessed.

## 1. Verdict

The base is very good: specific transition lists, `scale(.96)` on press, ink-on-magenta CTAs at 6.04:1, logical properties, balance/pretty wrapping, and a poster that equals frame 0 and the loop's last frame. Four things break the polish now:
- the Arabic **"وGoogle TV"** joins, which read as "TVg" on every AR surface;
- the **404 CTA**, which renders cream on magenta (2.89:1);
- the **AR reel scene**, where the joining phone covers the lobby;
- the **hero loop**, which is encoded BT.601 but tagged BT.709, so the colours jump at the poster → video cross-fade (coral #FF3356 → #FF4854, jade #2CC49E → #1EB19D).

Most of the rest is 1–3 line CSS fixes.

> Capture artifacts, so nobody misreports them: (a) the 390 "fold" shots show the page mid-scroll because `html{scroll-behavior:smooth}` (site.css:45) makes the harness's `scrollTo(0,0)` animate. (b) The hero hint "Connect this computer to the TV" on 390 shots comes from Playwright's desktop UA, not a real-phone bug. (c) The sticky nav sitting mid-hero in the full-page 390 shots is the same scroll artifact.

## 3. Keep (already excellent)

1. **Hero video architecture**: the poster equals frame 0 and the last frame (verified, seamless loop). The video is 1px inset, so the LCP stays the poster. It loads only after `load` + idle, pauses off-screen, and respects `saveData` and reduced motion. The pause toggle has correct `aria-pressed`, and its play glyph is not mirrored. Only the colour matrix (#4) needs fixing.
2. **Buttons**: ink on magenta (6.04:1), `scale(.96)` on press, explicit `transition-property` lists (no `transition: all` anywhere), layered inset highlight/shadow instead of borders, and 44px hit areas via pseudo-elements on the language pills.
3. **RTL mechanics**: logical properties throughout, `←` for RTL inline arrows, the reel progress bar's origin flips, Western digits, room codes and URLs in LTR isolates, and an Arabic wordmark swap (site and M6 end card). The M6 end-card lobby is mirrored exactly per DESIGN §10.
4. **Type system**: `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on paragraphs, antialiasing, `font-display: swap` + preload, an Arabic-capable Cairo subset at ~27 KB per weight, and Latin digits tabular by default (all advance 554), so the counters don't jitter.
5. **End cards (ads)**: CTA held still ≈ 4.8 s in the y 1110–1440 safe zone, a typeable URL pill, no fake QR. The visual hierarchy is consistent from wordmark to lobby to CTA.
