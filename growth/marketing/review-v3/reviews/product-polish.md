# Product polish review: make-interfaces-feel-better lens

Target: phone controller (`web-client/src/screens`, `styles.css`, `styles/tokens.css`), browser TV (`web-client/src/tv-mock`, `tv.css`), native TV (`tv-app/.../ui`). Skill checklist applied: concentric radii, optical alignment, shadows vs borders, split/stagger enters, soft exits, tabular numbers, font smoothing, text wrapping, image outlines, scale on press, skipping the enter animation on remount, no `transition: all`, `will-change`, 40 px hit areas. I also checked haptics, sound-to-animation sync, safe areas and TV focus.

## 1. Verdict
The motion system is well built. The tokens match one to one between `tokens.css` and `MishMotion.kt`, timers tick on a shared 5 Hz clock, reduced motion is handled everywhere, and there is no `transition: all` or `will-change` anywhere. The problems are at the edges:
- **Bugs:** (a) iPhone safe-area padding is applied twice, so every phone screen scrolls and the sticky top bar slides under the notch. (b) The eliminated player's phone buzzes and says "You're out" about 2.4 s before the TV's OUT stamp lands.
- **Polish:** nested radii run the wrong way (the avatar is rounder than its row). Full-width TV rows jump sideways when focused. Some text breaks leave one word alone on the last line (an orphan). The stamp and tick sounds play up to 150–200 ms before what they belong to. The web TV and the native TV have drifted apart on four motion values.

## 3. Keep (already excellent)
1. **One motion vocabulary on both stacks.** `tokens.css:89-94` and `MishMotion.kt:13-21` hold the same 80/150/240/400/800 ms values and the same four cubic-beziers. The native phase transition (`AppRoot.kt:314-315`) is exactly the DESIGN spec: enter 400 ms decel + 24 dp rise, exit 240 ms accel + scale 0.98.
2. **Timers.** The shared 5 Hz `now` signal ticks only while a component reads it (`Timer.tsx:15-24`). The bar fill transitions `transform 200ms linear` to match the tick, so it depletes smoothly without layout work. `.timer`/`.num` isolate the digits LTR and use tabular figures, and Cairo's digits are tabular by design (`MishType.kt:103-104`).
3. **Hold-to-reveal privacy timing.** A 150 ms threshold before the flip, a 10 ms haptic at the threshold, and an instant hide on release (`HoldToReveal.tsx:15,35`; `styles.css:419-438`). It is tactile and safe.
4. **The TV focus treatment.** Ring with a 2 px bg gap, then a 5 px cream ring, then an 18 px primary glow, on `motion.fast` with `ease.standard`. The scoreboard uses an inset ring so the clipping box cannot cut it. It reads instantly from the couch (tv-02-lobby-12-players.png "Karim", tv-14-dialog-kick-midgame.png "Cancel").
5. **Hygiene.** No `transition: all`, no `will-change`, specific property lists everywhere. Every tap target is ≥ 44–48 px (`--tap-min`, the `.chipbtn` 48 px hit area around a 36 px pill at `styles.css:82-84`). Reduced motion is covered element by element (`styles.css:675-686`, `tv.css:453-464`), and it halves the reveal times.
