# Product review: UI/UX Pro Max lens (user experience)

Target: phone controller (`web-client/src/screens`), browser TV (`web-client/src/tv-mock`), native TV (`tv-app/.../ui/screens`, which mirrors /tv).
Method: `ui-ux-pro-max` checklist (accessibility, touch and interaction, feedback and loading states, onboarding, empty states, copy), applied while walking the game as a first-time group (a host on the TV plus 5 friends who have never played), using the v3 screenshot pack and the code.
Copy is in EN. FR is given where it is short. Every new key needs FR and AR from the translator before it ships.

## 1. Verdict

The moment-to-moment flow is very good. On every phone it is clear what to do now: "Your turn!" fills the screen, the vote uses select-then-lock, "Vote locked. Act natural." confirms it, and the HoldToReveal card is easy to discover. The TV always says what phase it is in.
The gaps are in comprehension, not in mechanics. Nothing in the flow explains the core twist: that one word holder secretly has a different word. A first-time Mole cannot learn that they might be the odd one out.
The payoff moments are also under-built. The tie explanation is covered by a button and gone after 2.5 s. The TV verdict never says what the Blank guessed. The results screen does not say who the Mole was until you scroll the scoreboard.
The browser-TV entry has one real trap: Backspace or Esc in the lobby closes the room with no confirm. There is also no laptop-specific guidance.
Fix the 7 P1 items (all S/M effort) before investing in more visual polish.

## 3. Keep list (excellent, do not regress)

1. **HoldToReveal plus "Tap to show for 5 s instead"** (`ph-04-reveal-hidden.png`). The affordance is clear (hand icon, "Press and hold to see your word"). It has an accessible alternative, and "Got it" stays disabled until the card has been seen once.
2. **Full-bleed "Your turn!" in the player's colour with a giant DONE** (`ph-06-your-turn.png`). It is impossible to miss and has the right size of target for a nervous moment. The "Hold to peek my word" escape hatch is also right.
3. **Select-then-lock voting with the name in the CTA** ("Lock my vote: Eli"), then "Vote locked. Act natural. You voted for [Eli]" (`ph-07-vote-selected.png`, `ph-08-voted-locked.png`). This is ideal error prevention and confirmation.
4. **The TV ActionPill double-press** ("Press OK again to confirm") on Skip / Close vote / Start now. It prevents an accidental Enter from wrecking a turn. Keep it; just localise the hint for the browser (#17).
5. **Join flow and states**: the code boxes with an invalid-character shake, the hatched taken colours with a live preview tile, "You're in! Look at the TV: your tile is up.", the "Reconnecting… Your seat is saved." overlay, and "No room called QQQQ" with a single recovery CTA (`ph-02-join-filled.png`, `ph-16-reconnecting-ingame.png`, `ph-14-room-gone-not-found.png`).
