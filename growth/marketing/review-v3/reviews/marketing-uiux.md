# Marketing: UI/UX Pro Max (conversion UX) review, v3

Scope: landing `growth/site/` (EN/FR/AR at 390 and 1440, pre-launch build `dist/`, `content/prelaunch.json`), the Play listing (`growth/marketing/play-store/LISTING.md`, `GRAPHICS-SPEC.md`, `frames.tsv`, `src/*.html`, `graphics/*`), and the video end cards (`shots/video/*-endcard.png`, `growth/video/src/ads/EndCard.tsx`).
Persona: someone taps a TikTok/Reels ad at 10 pm, on their phone, inside the in-app browser (about 390 x 670 css px usable once TikTok's top bar and bottom toolbar are drawn), with friends on the couch and a TV in the room.

Capture caveat: the `*-390-fold.png` shots are not scroll 0. `landing.mjs` scrolls back with `scrollTo(0,0)` and waits 800 ms, but `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` (site.css:45) had not finished scrolling. That is why the sticky nav sits in the middle of the full-page shots (EN about 475 css px down, FR about 750). Below, I measured the fold from the full-page shots, where the top of the image is the true top of the page.

## 1. Verdict

- **3-second test: mostly passes in EN, fails in FR/AR.** The headline plus the PIZZA x5 / PASTA poster explain the game quickly. But the main CTA starts at about 570 css px in EN, about 657 in FR and about 645 in AR (bottom edges about 635, 720 and 705). That puts the FR and AR CTA below a TikTok in-app fold (about 670), and all three languages below the fold on small phones.
- **The phone-to-TV hand-off is the right idea but slow.** "Send the link to my laptop or TV" opens either the OS share sheet or a sheet with copy, email and "open here". It has no WhatsApp option (the main way people send themselves links in Lebanon/MENA), and it never tells the user the fastest path: type a short URL on the TV or laptop that is already in the room, then scan its QR with this phone. The URL `play.mishana.workers.dev/tv` is long, hard to type with a TV remote, and looks untrustworthy.
- **Hierarchy breaks lower down.** In the "Play in your browser" section, the cream primary button opens the TV game on the phone (a dead end). The "send" option there is only a ghost button.
- **Store and ads drift from the landing page.** The feature graphic is text-only in the old palette, although v2 decision #19 asked for TV + phones in palette B. There are two conflicting screenshot plans. The end cards say "Play free in your browser" to people who are already holding a browser on the wrong device.
- **Structure is sound.** The message-matched hero, the 15-second reel and the hold-to-peek demo are excellent. Fixes are mostly copy and reordering (S/M effort).
