## Rules (from the reviews — non-negotiable)
1. **Brightness**: no near-black frames; phone screens and tiles must read; never dim needed elements below 70 % (desaturate instead). The very first frame is the thumbnail: hook caption + main objects already composed (pre-settle with negative starts), and clearly lit (a colour field or bright objects).
2. **Pace**: every caption respects `holdFrames`; ≤ 2 lines; ≤ 1 new idea per beat; ≤ 1 SFX per beat (pops are auto-capped at 0.45 with pitch variation). Body length per DECISIONS / reviews (typically 13–16 s; the explainer and M7 may be ~18–20 s).
3. **Bigger**: hero elements ~30 % larger than v1; use the frame down to y ≈ 1600; clue bubbles ≥ 52 px; role/result lines ≥ 76 px; never put readable text inside a scaled-down TV.
4. **Say "TV" early**: a TV (or the TV lobby) on screen within the first 3 s.
5. **One full-screen colour flash** at the twist only, held ≤ 10 frames. Floods clear the farthest corner in ~0.35 s. No crossfades, no fade-ins, no 3D. Everything is a pure function of the frame (no CSS transitions).
6. **Game accuracy**: the phone word screen is `WordFace` (dark screen + player-colour card); the Mole isn't told — point it out only OUTSIDE its phone (amber ring/caption); the TV stamps **OUT** (−8°, amber) then "{name} was the Mole"; voting happens on phones. Claims allowed only: free to play, 3–12 players, no app on phones, no controllers, EN/FR/AR, playable in a browser now, Google TV app coming soon. No ratings, no user counts, no competitor names.
7. **Copy**: short, concrete, viewer-centred; exact copy suggestions are in `uiux.md` — use them unless you have a clearly better line.

## Process
Write your files only (named in your task). `npx tsc -p .` after each edit (a render queue may run in parallel — never leave the project uncompilable). Render ~8 key stills with `npx remotion still src/index.ts <ID> /tmp/claude-1000/-home-alex/fd33f8b8-ff3a-4b57-a939-83c108a2f362/scratchpad/v2/<ad>/f-<n>.png --frame=<n> --props='{"blur":0,"guide":true}' --log=error`, LOOK at them, fix, iterate ≥ 2 times (always check frame 0). Then a draft without blur (`npx remotion render src/index.ts <ID> …/draft.mp4 --props='{"blur":0,"guide":false}' --concurrency=3 --log=error`), measure brightness (`ffmpeg -i draft.mp4 -vf signalstats,metadata=print:key=lavfi.signalstats.YAVG -f null - 2>&1 | grep YAVG` → target average 45–60) and step through fast moments with ffmpeg frame strips. No motion-blur renders. Return: a beat map (beat → event → caption → SFX → VO line), average luma, caption hold check (every caption's on-screen seconds), draft path, open issues.
