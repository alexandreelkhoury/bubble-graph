1. Jackbox Party Packs: the biggest catalogue

Each pack bundles five games (drawing, writing jokes, trivia, bluffing). The TV runs the game and players join on their phone, tablet or laptop at jackbox.tv, no controllers needed. On Google Play: Party Packs 1 to 7 and 10 run on Android TV (9to5Google); Packs 1–4 cost $24.99, Packs 5–7 and 10 cost $29.99. Packs 8 and 9 are not on Google Play. Pack 10 supports up to 9 players plus an audience and five languages (English, French, Italian, German, Spanish); most earlier packs are English only.

  • Best for: groups who want variety and big laughs, 3 to 8 players.
  • Keep in mind: each pack is a separate purchase. A free, ad-supported Jackbox TV app was announced in 2025 but had not launched as of this update.

2. AirConsole: a free console made of phones

AirConsole by N-Dream is a free Android TV app; phones become controllers through its controller app. The free tier offers a weekly rotation of games for 2 players with ads. The Hero subscription removes ads and unlocks all games, and one subscriber unlocks the session for everyone. Prices vary by store and region, so check in the app.

  • Best for: trying lots of casual multiplayer games with family.
  • Keep in mind: the free tier is limited to 2 players; parties need Hero.

3. Mish Ana!: secret-word bluffing for big groups

Mish Ana! is a secret-word party game for Android TV and Google TV: 3–12 players join from their phones by scanning a QR code, get a secret word, and find the player whose word is different. The Mole has a close word without knowing it; from 5 players a Blank has no word and bluffs. One spoken clue each, a vote on the phones, a reveal on the TV. Phones play in the browser (iPhone or Android, nothing to install).

  • Best for: 5 to 12 players, families, fans of “who’s the spy” / Mr. White-style games, and Arabic or French speakers (English, French and Arabic with Lebanese word packs).
  • Price: free to play, no ads, no accounts.
  • Keep in mind: one game type only (not a variety pack), and it needs internet on the TV and every phone. A free browser version runs on any TV through a laptop: {{BROWSER_SHORT}}.

4. Tower of Babel

A free co-op stacking game from the AirConsole team, available as its own Android TV app, one smartphone per player, with ads.

  • Best for: a quick, wordless warm-up with kids.

5. KaraFun and Singa: karaoke night

KaraFun works on Android TV (also Apple TV and Fire TV); guests scan a QR code on the TV to add songs from their phones, and it has singing battles and music quizzes. Free song selection, full catalogue by subscription. Singa also has an Android TV app, free with a Premium subscription and a 30-day trial.

  • Best for: birthdays and groups who would rather sing than bluff.

6. Codenames Online

The official, free browser version of Codenames by Czech Games Edition at codenames.game. There is no TV app: open it on a laptop connected to the TV, and players can join the room from their own devices.

  • Best for: two teams of word lovers, 4 to 8 players.

7. Drawing games in the browser: skribbl.io and Gartic Phone

skribbl.io (draw and guess, private rooms of 2–20) and Gartic Phone (the drawing telephone game, free with ads) run in a browser. Put a laptop on the TV for the shared view; everyone plays on their own device.

Popular, but not on Android TV

  • Just Dance Now (Ubisoft) has no Android TV app; Ubisoft suggests the Puffin browser on Android TV, or casting with Chromecast (FAQ).
  • Heads Up!, Psych!, Kahoot! and UNO! are phone apps on Google Play, with no TV version.
  • Spyfall and Undercover-style word games: we found no TV versions (see games like Spyfall and Undercover).

Which TVs can install these games?

Any TV or streamer with Google TV or Android TV and the Google Play Store: many Sony, TCL and Hisense models, older Philips models, the Google TV Streamer, and the Chromecast with Google TV (no longer sold, still supported). What is the best free party game for Android TV? For a whole group without paying: Mish Ana! (free to play, 3–12 players, secret-word bluffing) and AirConsole’s free tier (2 players, with ads). Can I use my phone as a controller on Google TV? Yes, in party games built for it: Jackbox and Mish Ana! use the phone’s browser, AirConsole uses its controller app. Nothing needs to be paired over Bluetooth. What party game works for 12 players on a TV? Most Jackbox games stop at 8 players (Pack 10 goes to 9 plus an audience). Mish Ana! supports 12 players, each on their own phone. Karaoke apps have no player limit. Do these games work on a Samsung or LG TV? Not from Google Play. Use the browser versions on a laptop connected with HDMI: Mish Ana!’s browser version, Codenames Online, skribbl.io or Gartic Phone.
