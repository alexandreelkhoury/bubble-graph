Key facts

The name

“Mish Ana!” (مش أنا!) is Lebanese Arabic for “Not me!”, which is exactly what every player says when the group accuses them. In French it’s « Pas moi ! ».

How a game works

The TV shows a QR code and a room code. Each player scans it and joins in their phone’s browser with a nickname. Everyone gets a secret word on their own phone; the Mole’s word is slightly different and the Blank has none. Players give one spoken clue each, vote on their phones, and the TV reveals the role of the player voted out. Full rules: how to play (en français).
