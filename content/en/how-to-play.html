In short: everyone gets a secret word on their own phone. Most players (the Civilians) share the same word, the Mole gets a slightly different one without being told, and in groups of 5 or more a Blank gets no word at all. Each player says one clue out loud, then everyone votes on their phone. The TV reveals the role of the player voted out. Civilians win by voting out every Mole and Blank; the others win by surviving.

Mish Ana! (Lebanese for “Not me!”) is played with one TV and one phone per player, for 3 to 12 players. A round takes a few minutes and the first one teaches the rules. This page covers everything: setup, roles, a full round, ties, how the game ends, points and tips for each role.

What you need

  • A TV screen: the Mish Ana! app on an Android TV or Google TV, or the browser version ({{BROWSER_SHORT}}) on a laptop connected to any TV.
  • One phone per player, iPhone or Android, with a camera and internet. Nothing to install: the game opens in the phone’s browser.
  • 3 to 12 players in the same room. The game is spoken: clues are said out loud.

Setup in 30 seconds

  1. Open Mish Ana! on the TV. It shows a QR code and a four-letter room code.
  2. Everyone scans the QR code with their phone camera, types a nickname and picks a colour.
  3. The host (the TV, or the first player) checks the settings and presses Start.

The roles

Nobody is told “you are a Civilian” or “you are the Mole”: you only see a word. That is the whole game.

Civilian

Has the majority word, for example PIZZA. Goal: find and vote out the infiltrators (the Mole and the Blank) without giving the word away.

Mole

Has a slightly different word, for example PASTA, and does not know it is different. Goal: work out from the clues that their word is the odd one, then blend in and survive.

Blank

Gets no word, and knows it. Goal: listen, bluff a believable clue and survive. If voted out, the Blank gets one guess at the Civilians’ word: a correct guess wins the game for the Blank alone.

How many Moles and Blanks?

By default the game decides from the number of players. The host can also pick them by hand (1 to 5 Moles, 0 to 2 Blanks, always fewer infiltrators than Civilians).

A round, step by step

  1. Secret words. Each phone shows a face-down card. Press and hold to see your word, let go to hide it. Your word never appears on the TV while you play.
  2. Clues. The TV picks who starts (never the Blank) and shows whose turn it is. Each player says one word or a short phrase out loud that relates to their word, without saying the word. By default each turn has a 45-second timer.
  3. Vote. Everyone votes on their phone for the player who sounds off. You can’t vote for yourself. The default vote timer is 90 seconds.
  4. Reveal. The player with the most votes is out. The TV stamps them OUT and reveals their role: Civilian, Mole or Blank.
  5. Next round. If nobody has won yet, the remaining players give new clues and vote again.

Ties

If two or more players tie for the most votes, only they give one more clue each, then everyone votes again between them. If the re-vote ties again, the game picks one of them at random (the host can change this to “nobody is out”).

The Blank’s last chance

When the Blank is voted out, they type one guess at the Civilians’ word on their phone. Spelling doesn’t need to be perfect, and the host can accept or reject a close answer. Right: the Blank wins alone. Wrong: the game goes on.

How the game ends

  • Civilians win when every Mole and Blank has been voted out.
  • The infiltrators win (Moles and Blanks together) under the default Official rule when only one Civilian is left, or under the Parity rule as soon as they are as many as the Civilians.
  • The Blank wins alone by guessing the Civilians’ word after being voted out.

Scoring

Every player on the winning side scores, even if they were voted out earlier. Default points:

  • Civilian: 2 points each
  • Mole: 10 points each
  • Blank: 6 points

Scores carry over when you press Play again, so a party night becomes a league. The host can set each value from 0 to 20.

Tips for each role

As a Civilian

  • Give clues that prove you know the word without making it easy to guess. For PIZZA, “slice” is fine; “Italian round thing with cheese” hands the word to the Blank.
  • Listen for clues that fit two words. “Fork” fits pasta better than pizza.
  • Don’t rush the first vote: a vague clue can come from a careful Civilian.

As the Mole

  • You don’t know you’re the Mole. If the first clues feel slightly off for your word, you probably are.
  • Pick clues that fit both your word and the one you suspect the others have.
  • Vote with the group when it’s safe: a lone vote draws attention.

As the Blank

  • Try not to speak early. Build your clue from the ones you’ve heard.
  • Stay general (“I’d have it on a Friday night”) and keep a guess ready in case you’re caught.

Settings worth knowing

  • Beginner mode shows everyone their role, which is great for a first game with kids or newcomers.
  • Family-friendly filter keeps only word packs rated for everyone.
  • Timers for clues, votes and the Blank’s guess, or none: the host moves things on.
  • Difficulty from Easy to Subtle, and word packs in English, French and Arabic, including Lebanese packs.
