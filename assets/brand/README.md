# JAMAA brand assets

Source vectors for the **JAMAA** (جماعة) platform identity, built per [DESIGN-JAMAA.md](../../docs/DESIGN-JAMAA.md) §1.3–§1.4 and [JAMAA-PLATFORM.md](../../docs/JAMAA-PLATFORM.md) §3.9. All text is converted to outlines from **Cairo Black** (SIL OFL 1.1, © The Cairo Project Authors; the same TTFs ship in `tv-app/app/src/main/res/font/`). Everything here is generated by `scripts/gen_brand.py`: do not edit the files by hand.

Generated Android resources: `tv-app/app/src/main/res/drawable/banner.xml` (EN/FR), `drawable-ar/banner.xml`, `drawable/ic_mark.xml` (in-app mark on its rounded tile), `drawable/ic_launcher_background.xml`, `ic_launcher_foreground.xml`, `ic_launcher_monochrome.xml` (Android 13 themed icon: the five bodies and the dot as one flat layer), and the Compose wordmark geometry `tv-app/app/src/main/java/app/jamaa/tv/ui/components/BrandPaths.kt` (drawn by `Wordmark` in `ui/components/Brand.kt`).

Regenerate everything after changing the construction (`--preview DIR` also renders PNG previews with a headless Chromium for review):

```sh
pip install fonttools uharfbuzz skia-pathops
python3 assets/brand/scripts/gen_brand.py --preview /tmp/jamaa-brand-preview
```

The script prints the measured construction: for each A the crossbar cut (`A*x`, `A*w`) and the head centre (`A*cx`), the Arabic size and the ج dot, and the solved ring layout. It stops with an error if a crossbar fragment is left, a head overlaps the letters, or the lamp touches a body.

## Where the generator departs from the spec's numbers

DESIGN-JAMAA §1.3–§1.4 give target numbers that do not fit Cairo Black's real outlines or each other, so the script measures and solves instead (the intent stays: open A's with a head under each roof; five bodies touching around a lamp, inside the safe zone):

- **Crossbar band.** Cairo Black's A crossbar sits at 17–37 % of the cap height (the spec searches 55–75 %). The script finds it on the A's axis (the first ink run above the baseline) and cuts along the legs' inner edges.
- **Head size and height.** The spec's r 19 at baseline − 62 lands on the legs: Cairo Black's counter is 31.7 units wide at that height. The heads sit at baseline − 52 with the largest radius that keeps 3 units of clearance (r 14.1). The ج dot keeps r 19 (it sits outside the letters).
- **Cap height.** 220-unit Cairo Black caps are 149 units tall (the spec estimates ≈ 158); the Arabic size follows the measured value (alef = cap height, ≈ 212.8 units of font size).
- **Ring.** 17-unit bodies on a radius-19 circle leave about 9 units between neighbours, so they cannot touch. Each body slides along its ray (−90°, −18°, 54°, 126°, 198°) toward the lamp until it meets a neighbour (1-unit overlap, cut by a 1.5-unit gap where they meet) or the lamp's gap; the body size is the largest that keeps the ring inside radius 29 (the 66-unit safe circle minus margin), and the group is re-centred on the tile.

Game #1's own art (the "shared bang" wordmark and the speech-bubble icon) is game art now, with its own generator under `assets/games/<id>/` (JAMAA-PLATFORM §3.9).
